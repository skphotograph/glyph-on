# Third-party software and icons / 第三者のソフトウェアとアイコン

Distributed builds include Electron and Chromium. Their upstream notices are included in the application distribution as `LICENSE.electron.txt` and `LICENSES.chromium.html` (the Electron license filename may vary by build). The notices in the installed JavaScript dependencies, including electron-updater and js-yaml, apply to those components. The exact dependency versions and sources are listed in `package-lock.json`.

Glyph-on's default mark and built-in glyphs are part of the Glyph-on source. Automatically retrieved app icons belong to their respective rights holders. Custom icons are provided by the individual user.

配布版にはElectronとChromiumが含まれ、それぞれのライセンス文書を同梱します。electron-updater、js-yamlを含むJavaScript依存関係には各パッケージのライセンスが適用されます。詳細なバージョンと取得元は `package-lock.json` を参照してください。自動取得したアプリアイコンの権利は各権利者に帰属し、カスタムアイコンは利用者が選択した画像です。
